Tools

Tools for the day-to-day

Small, calm tools made with neurodivergent people. Free to use, and your data stays on your device.

Maze Cat

Your task list and a timer that floats over the taskbar. Pick a task, press Start, and it stays in front of you until it's done. When time's up, the cat wakes up and a soft chime plays.

Windows. Open source (MIT license). Your lists are plain files on your computer.

Get Maze Cat on GitHub

More on the way

Quiet modes that pause notifications when it's all too much, help turning a long email into steps, and a shared look across every tool so nobody has to relearn anything.

BrightPost is a guide, not a clinic. We aren't licensed psychologists, doctors or lawyers, and nothing on this site is a diagnosis, treatment or medical, psychological or legal advice. We point you to the people and organizations who can help. Always check details with them, since services and phone numbers change.

In danger right now? Call 911. In a mental-health crisis, call or text 988, or chat at chat.988lifeline.org.

BrightPost is planned as a program of GOVW Foundation, Inc. No cookies, no trackers, no third-party scripts. Text set in Atkinson Hyperlegible, made by the Braille Institute for easy reading.
