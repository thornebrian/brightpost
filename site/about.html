About

About BrightPost

BrightPost started with one person's own tools. Its founder built Maze Cat to stay on track, and it made enough of a difference to share. BrightPost grows that into a home for the neurodivergent community and the people who support it: clear guides, trusted links and calm tools.

A lighthouse doesn't steer the ship. It shows where the rocks are and where the harbor is, so you can choose your own way. That's the job we want to do.

Who runs it

BrightPost is planned as a program of GOVW Foundation, Inc., a nonprofit. We're building a team that includes neurodivergent people, families and educators, and we'll introduce them here as they join.

What we are, and what we aren't

  • We are a guide: we explain the process in plain words and point you to the right people.
  • We aren't licensed psychologists, doctors or lawyers. We don't diagnose, treat or give medical, psychological or legal advice.
  • We don't accept payment for listing organizations.

Your privacy

This site has no cookies, no trackers and no third-party scripts. A full privacy policy comes with the help desk.
