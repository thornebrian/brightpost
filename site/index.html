Different wiring, real strengths

We see neurodivergence as a difference, not a defect. Many people find strengths in the way their mind works, and we build for those strengths instead of trying to change people.

How we work

  • Nothing about us without us. Neurodivergent people help design and test everything here.
  • Self-identification counts. You don't need a diagnosis to use our guides or tools.
  • Calm by default. Quiet colors, no pop-ups, no flashing, no surprise sounds.
  • At no cost. The guides and tools are open to anyone who needs them.
  • Your data stays yours. This site doesn't track you.

BrightPost is a guide, not a clinic. We aren't licensed psychologists, doctors or lawyers, and nothing on this site is a diagnosis, treatment or medical, psychological or legal advice. We point you to the people and organizations who can help. Always check details with them, since services and phone numbers change.

In danger right now? Call 911. In a mental-health crisis, call or text 988, or chat at chat.988lifeline.org.
